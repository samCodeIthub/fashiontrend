01. ABOUT US

A house built on thread, not trends

JOBEQS Thread and Trend takes its name from the founder's parents —
Joseph and Rebecca — with Quality and Style built into every letter after.
"Thread and Trend" is the promise: we work thread by thread, tailoring and
sewing with care, to create pieces that set the trend rather than chase it.

Behind every collection is the same dress form pictured in our mark —
patient, precise, and wound in the same red-pink thread that runs through
this entire site.
